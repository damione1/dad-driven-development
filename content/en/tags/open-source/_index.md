[Soufflé]({{< ref "/blog/souffle-local-transcription-macos/" >}}) explores local transcription on macOS. The [Maslow controller]({{< ref "/blog/maslow-desktop-cnc-controller/" >}}) connects desktop software to a CNC workflow.
